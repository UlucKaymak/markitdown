The Ways of

Collaboration

Last updated: June 2025

Scope

- All commissioned work is defined by the brief mutually agreed upon before the project begins.
- Additional requests or significant changes beyond the agreed scope may require a re-evaluation of the timeline and budget.

Payment

- Base rate: $75 (unless otherwise negotiated)
- Upfront: 50% payment required before project begins ((for first-time clients) don’t take this personal))
- Final: Remaining 50% due upon delivery
- Consulting rate: $10/hour for advisory or brainstorming sessions
- All payments should be made via bank transfer, Stripe, BuyMeACoffee, or Crypto.
- Late payments (14+ days) may result in delivery delays for ongoing or future projects.

Revisions

- Projects include up to 3 revision rounds.
- Further changes beyond that will be charged additionally.
- If revisions deviate significantly from the original brief, a new budget may be proposed.

Delivery

- Delivery format (audio/video/code/image) is specified per project.
- Source files (if included) will be sent after full payment.
- Final files are shared via a downloadable link or a shared folder (WeTransfer, Drive, etc.).

Rights & Credits

- Unless specified otherwise, authorship is shared:
- “Sound design/Visuals/Coding by Uluç Kaymak” etc.
- Commercial usage rights must be discussed beforehand.
- I reserve the right to document and archive the work for my portfolio or exhibitions.

Cancellation

- If the project is canceled after starting, the upfront payment is non-refundable.
- If I cancel due to scheduling or health, a refund will be provided if no work has begun.

Personal Note

- I’m here to make meaningful and unique work. Let’s respect each other’s time, labor, and weird ideas.​
